I like animals too!

Bio About Me

I am someone who likes learning how living things and computer systems work. Animals are a big part of my life, especially my pet snakes, because they make me curious about behavior, care, habitats, and the way different species survive. I also like plants because they connect to that same idea of growth and patience. Taking care of animals and plants has taught me to pay attention to small details, and that same skill helps me when I code.

My coding interests started with Scratch games, websites, and programs where I could make something move, react, or change based on what the user does. I especially like evolution games because they combine animals, survival, creativity, and logic. I enjoy building projects that feel alive, like games where creatures adapt or websites that show my interests in a fun way. Outside of coding, I work at Culver's, which helps me practice responsibility, teamwork, and staying focused when things get busy. I also have an upcoming internship at Catapult, and I am excited because it gives me a chance to learn more about technology, real work environments, and how the skills I am building now can turn into bigger opportunities later. Food, animals, plants, and coding may seem like different interests, but they all connect to who I am: I like making things, caring for things, and learning how things grow.

Plans for the Future

In the future, I want to keep building my education around coding, technology, and creative problem solving. I am interested in learning more about web design, game development, and programs that use logic to create systems that feel alive, like evolution games or simulations. I also want to keep improving my skills with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and other tools that can help me turn ideas into finished projects. My upcoming internship at Catapult is an important step because it can help me see what technology work is like outside of school and give me more experience working with real projects and real expectations.

For my career, I would like to do something that lets me build, design, and solve problems. I could see myself making websites, games, apps, or other programs that people can actually use. I also want a career that gives me enough stability to support my interests, take care of my animals, and keep learning new things. Later in life, I would like to be in a place where I have worked hard enough to be comfortable, have time for hobbies, and maybe even build projects just because I think they are interesting. Retirement for me would not mean stopping completely. It would mean having the freedom to choose what I make, what I learn, and how I spend my time.
